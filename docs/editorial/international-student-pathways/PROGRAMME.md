# International student post-graduation pathways

Research cut-off 26 November 2026. UK immigration rules have UK-wide scope. Employment markets, professional regulators and student services vary by nation, institution and sector. This series serves final-year students, recent graduates and their families. It uses plain-language equivalents for PSW, CoS, eVisa, dependants and settlement in Simplified and Traditional Chinese, English, Urdu and Hindi. Passport-specific routes are signposted only where the official eligibility makes nationality relevant.
