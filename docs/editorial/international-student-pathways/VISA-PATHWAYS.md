# UK post-study routes compared

Rules and guidance reviewed 26 November 2026. This is a decision framework for editorial use. The exact sponsor, occupation, permission and family circumstances determine eligibility. A route's current entry rules and the future rules for settlement are separate checks. Use the [source register](SOURCE-REGISTER.md) for primary references.

| Route | What makes it realistic | Questions or limits to check |
|---|---|---|
| Graduate | In-country, qualifying course and UK study, qualifying education provider and reported successful completion. No sponsoring employer needed. | Apply before Student permission expires. Non-doctoral 2 years for valid applications on or before 31 December 2026, 18 months from 1 January 2027. Doctoral 3 years. The clock starts at decision. No extension or direct settlement. Limited dependant eligibility. |
| Skilled Worker | Actual eligible offer, appropriately licensed employer, electronic CoS, eligible occupation, enough qualifying salary and going rate, and applicable English requirement. | For in-country Student switch, see SW 1.5A. Check course completion, a qualifying job start after the course end, or the qualifying full-time doctoral exception. Graduate holders can switch if eligible. For standard cases compare £41,700 and the full going rate, and check permitted exceptions and transitional rules separately. New entrants typically need at least £33,400 and 70% of the relevant going rate under option E plus a qualifying reason. SW 12.3 caps combined Skilled Worker, Graduate and Tier 2 permission at four years for new entrant eligibility, including discontinuous periods. A licence never guarantees an offer or sponsorship for a particular team. |
| Health and Care Worker | Check the actual role code, approved sponsor, applicable salary or national pay-scale requirements, and registration with the regulator where required. Separate NHS jobs, regulated professions and adult social care. | Eligible applicants pay no immigration health surcharge. Overseas entry for care workers and senior care workers (SOC 6135/6136) closed on 22 July 2025. Check the limited in-country rules and transitional deadline in SW 6.1B and sponsor guidance, including lawful work for the sponsor for at least three months where that condition applies. Transitional applications under SW 6.1B(a) must be made before 22 July 2028. In England check the applicable CQC registration requirement. Check dependants separately. |
| Innovator Founder | Approved endorsing body and a credible new, innovative, viable and scalable business. Check Student switching and course requirements. | Business ownership and the right to work in the business are separate. “Self-sponsorship” is a commercial label. If a company seeks to sponsor a worker it must satisfy the genuine role and sponsor requirements. Do not buy a CoS or rely on company incorporation. |
| Global Talent | Eligible leadership/potential in research, academia, arts, culture or digital technology, usually endorsement or an eligible prize. | An ordinary degree does not by itself demonstrate eligibility. Some research appointments or approved fellowships can be relevant. Review route-specific evidence. |
| High Potential Individual | Eligible overseas qualification within five years, checked against the list for the award date. | UK universities are excluded from the eligible list. Check the annual cap, prior permissions and switch restrictions before relying on a separate overseas degree. |
| Scale-up | Specific Scale-up sponsor, eligible offer, sponsorship certificate and pay. | Initial sponsored role must meet the six-month condition. Later work flexibility and settlement pay history are separate assessments. |
| India Young Professionals Scheme, Youth Mobility and BN(O) | Nationality and other requirements differ. The India route uses a ballot, and an initial Youth Mobility application must be made from abroad. | These are independent routes. Neither Indian heritage nor study in the UK meets passport/eligibility conditions. For Hong Kong, check BN(O) and family eligibility against that route. For Pakistani or mainland Chinese passport holders, check individual eligibility rather than assuming access to a mobility scheme. |
| Eligible partner or dependant | Existing genuine relationship or dependant circumstances meeting the actual route's sponsor and requirements. | Family and work-route dependant permissions differ. Make a separate calendar and check each person's work and travel conditions. |
| Further study | Credible programme and funding, an eligible Student sponsor and CAS where required. | Verify in-country eligibility, timing and academic progression conditions and exceptions with the university before paying a deposit. Budget tuition, maintenance and IHS separately. Graduate permission restricts study on courses eligible under the Student route at Student sponsors. |
| Outside the UK | Parallel job search, university alumni and references, professional credentials and a budget for resettling. | UK work from abroad depends on employer policy and local laws. Keep immigration and employment records for any later eligible UK application. Plan to leave before expiry if no lawful basis to remain. |

## Comparing dates and risks

- Keep course end, official completion reporting, Student expiry, any revised UKVI notice and proposed sponsored start dates separate. A resit, graduation ceremony, offer or payment receipt does not establish completion reporting, a visa application or eligibility.
- Confirm salary, SOC code, job duties, weekly hours, visa costs and CoS timeline with HR through the employer's official channels. Start the careers search before results. Many structured schemes recruit in autumn, while ordinary vacancies and some schemes recruit year-round. Ask separately about present right to work and sponsorship later. Use realistic examples from projects, paid work or volunteering for interviews.
- Student with delayed results and permission expiring first: ask for the sponsor's timetable now. Seek university or regulated advice about genuine options and plan departure before expiry if no lawful basis exists. A saved application, job offer or unassigned CoS confers no status. An in-time valid application can engage section 3C if permission expires while undecided. Pending Student conditions and the specific permanent-vacancy exception require checking. Refusal, cancellation, travel, late or invalid applications require individual advice.
- Two years on Graduate and then three years as a Skilled Worker do not amount to five qualifying years under the current ordinary Skilled Worker settlement rules in SW 21.2. The same lawful periods may form part of a different ten-year long-residence calculation, subject to that route's permission, continuity, absences and other requirements. This example assumes regular valid grants, not historic transitional exceptions.
- The 12 November 2026 accessible earned-settlement response describes government policy. It is distinct from operative rules, and the revised framework should be applied only when and as corresponding rules commence. The scheduled Skilled Worker settlement B2 speaking/listening requirement from 26 March 2027 has a separate operative provision. Recheck both before release. Avoid claiming universal grandfathering.

## Fraud and hardship

Use the official register and contact the employer through a separately verified channel. Confirm the employing entity, role and HR contact in writing. Avoid personal payment requests to secure sponsorship. Workers may have official application fees of their own. The sponsor guidance identifies charges the employer cannot pass on. Save messages and receipts privately. For suspected visa fraud, use the official reporting path. In an exploitation case, approach regulated advice and specialist support before taking steps that could affect your position. A report does not change immigration status.
