# Coverage and search intent

Audited on 26 November 2026. The public Guide API snapshot contained 77 Guides in each of the five locales before Phase 3. Searched the backend editorial tree and open backend PRs alongside the public catalogue. The public NHS cluster focuses on accessing and paying for care. The earlier [catalogue audit](../../catalogue-expansion-2026-11/AUDIT.md) identified the dedicated Health and Care Worker Guide as a gap. No matching published slug or other corresponding draft package was found in the reviewed inventory. Check the protected draft inventory before any import.

| Reader intent | Existing coverage | Phase 3 boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Is an NHS or private healthcare vacancy eligible, and will this employer sponsor it? | Work Visa overview, occupation and salary, and sponsorship/CoS Guides explain the general rules. | Separate profession, actual advertised vacancy, licensed employer, correct pay rules and visa. Link back to the general switching and settlement Guides. |
| Which regulator handles a doctor, nurse, midwife or allied health professional? | NHS Guides cover patients and costs. The existing work-visa Guides do not give regulator-specific registration directions. | Explain GMC, NMC and HCPC starting points and ask about employer timing. Keep specialist pathways and regulator applications with the regulator. |
| Can I use a support role without a clinical qualification? | Graduate Visa and options-after-graduation Guides cover immigration permission generally. | Explain realistic support, data and administrative job searches. Distinguish the job's own requirements and its separate sponsorship eligibility. |
| Can I switch to a care-worker job after three months, including agency work? | Switching and occupation Guides give the general context. The dependants Guide covers family eligibility. | Apply SW 6.1B to actual employer, occupation and CoS dates. Explain distinct current-worker provisions and the CQC requirement. |
| What about expiry, settlement and scams? | Post-study options, Skilled Worker settlement and existing CoS guidance cover the wider decisions. | Surface the immediate care/healthcare checks and deep-link. Avoid repeating wider eligibility and application instructions. |

Both drafts use locale-aware links and the existing Work Visa disclaimer. The curated Work Visa hub should expose the two routes in its relevant topic before a frontend release. Coordinate that later change with the frontend owner and check it against the final frontend revision. This draft PR is backend editorial only.
