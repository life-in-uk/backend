# International student pathways: healthcare and adult social care

Phase 3 drafts cover two decisions. An international graduate or overseas-trained clinician looking at NHS or other healthcare jobs needs to check the vacancy, immigration eligibility and professional registration separately. A student or Graduate visa holder considering care work needs to check the two restricted occupation codes, lawful work history and family circumstances before relying on a switch.

| Guide | Reader decision | Category |
|---|---|---|
| `health-and-care-worker-visa` | Can this NHS or other healthcare vacancy qualify? Which regulator and salary table apply? | Work Visa |
| `adult-social-care-worker-sponsorship` | Can this particular 6135 or 6136 job support an in-country application? Does any exception apply to my employer or child? | Work Visa |

The visa rules apply across the UK. The CQC employer check is specific to England. Professional regulators and salary rules govern separate decisions. See the [coverage map](COVERAGE-MAP.md), [question evidence](QUESTION-RESEARCH.md), [source register](SOURCE-REGISTER.md) and [review](REVIEW.md).

The next research batch should look at what happens when a sponsor loses its licence or a worker needs to change employers, particularly in care. Start with the existing job-loss and employer-change Guides and pursue only practical gaps.
