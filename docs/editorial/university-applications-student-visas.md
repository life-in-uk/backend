## Source review and scope

Source review started 18 November 2026. Recheck dates, fees, thresholds and legal rules before approving each Guide and before rolling the admissions cycle forward.

- **UCAS cycle.** Most 2027-entry courses have an equal consideration deadline of 13 January 2027 at 18:00 UK time. Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses have the earlier 15 October 2026 deadline. Clearing opens 2 July 2027. The 2027 application fee is £34.50 for up to five choices under the standard undergraduate route. Confirm school deadlines, course-specific exceptions and Results Day for the applicant's qualification. Avoid carrying a 2026 A-level results date into 2027. [UCAS dates](https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/dates-and-deadlines-for-uni-applications) · [UCAS fee](https://www.ucas.com/faqs/what-is-the-application-fee-for-the-2027-cycle) · [Sending exam results](https://www.ucas.com/applying/after-you-apply/clearing-and-results-day/results-day/sending-exam-results)
- **Admissions.** Example requirements must use the exact course, provider, start year and qualification jurisdiction. Explain predicted grades, subject conditions, admissions tests, international qualifications and optional contextual consideration. Verify the statement's three questions, 350-character minimum per answer and 4,000-character aggregate including spaces. Explain that an offer or Clearing conversation is distinct from a confirmed place. [UCAS entry requirements](https://www.ucas.com/international/international-students/applying-to-university-as-an-international-student/entry-requirements-for-uk-courses) · [Personal statement](https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/writing-your-personal-statement/the-new-personal-statement-for-2026-entry) · [Offer replies](https://www.ucas.com/applying/after-you-apply/types-of-offers/replying-to-your-offers) · [Clearing](https://www.ucas.com/applying/after-you-apply/clearing-and-results-day/what-is-clearing)
- **Student Finance England.** Start with the course start date and home nation, then nationality, status and residence history. Most eligible new courses from 1 January 2027 use LLE. The autumn 2027 application opening is stated as spring 2027. Treat home-fee status, finance eligibility and Student Visa eligibility as three separate decisions. If citing £10,050 for 2027/28, state the English regulated standard full-time course and provider scope and check which fee-limit regulations apply. Quote overseas tuition only from a named course's current fee page. [LLE application service](https://www.gov.uk/apply-for-student-finance-lifelong-learning-entitlement) · [Eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/student-finance-on-or-after-1-january-2027/eligibility) · [When to apply](https://www.gov.uk/apply-for-student-finance-lifelong-learning-entitlement/when-to-apply) · [DfE fee limits](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tuition-fees-and-student-support-2026-to-2027-academic-year/changes-to-tuition-fees-2026-to-2027-academic-year-and-2027-to-2028-academic-year)
- **Student Visa.** First distinguish British and Irish citizens, settled applicants, applicants with an existing permission that allows study, and those needing Student permission. Verify the currently listed £558 application fee, typical three weeks from outside or eight weeks from inside the UK, and £776/year student IHS rate separately. IHS depends on permission length and exemptions. Verify the visa brake and switching exclusions in the current rules. Explain that universities and UKVI assess different requirements. [Student visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa) · [Documents](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/documents-you-must-provide) · [Appendix Student](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student) · [IHS](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay)
- **CAS and evidence.** Check sponsorship and CAS details against the sponsor guidance. As reviewed, the monthly maintenance figures were £1,529 in London and £1,171 elsewhere, up to nine months, in addition to outstanding course fees where applicable. Distinguish an exemption from showing bank evidence, a qualifying loan or official sponsorship, and differential evidence treatment. Check the 28-day holding period and 31-day evidence recency together. For English, check Appendix Student, Appendix English Language and what the sponsor assesses. [Sponsor duties](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/student-sponsor-guidance/sponsorship-duties-accessible) · [Appendix Finance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-finance) · [Financial evidence](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/financial-evidence-for-student-and-child-student-route-applicants) · [English requirements](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english)
- **Arrival and conditions.** Use the eVisa and linked passport instructions, the provider's enrolment procedure, and the student's individual work and study conditions. Explain statutory work limits, self-employment restrictions, changes in sponsorship or studies, and the narrow dependant categories. Do not describe an ordinary undergraduate offer as giving dependant eligibility. Cover relevant government sponsorship and continuing-dependant exceptions from the rules. [Travel with an eVisa](https://www.gov.uk/evisa/travel-with-evisa) · [Partner and children](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/family-members) · [UKVI caseworker guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/student-route-caseworker-guidance/student-and-child-student)
