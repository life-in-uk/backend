# Optional future commercial placements

Checked 21 November 2026. These are planning notes. The draft files contain only editorial links. The owner controls any later partner relationship and product approval.

| Topic | Where a placement could sit | Editorial boundary | Required review before adopting it |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIM-only and broadband | After the total-cost and coverage checklist | Keep coverage tools, switching instructions and alternatives freely available. State any minimum term and which fees or bundles are included. | Obtain provider permission for programme, creative, markets and terms. Identify the commercial material before engagement. Check separately for regulated handset finance. |
| Energy switching | After the tariff and moving sections | Explain region, payment method, standing charge and unit rate. Avoid comparisons based on a single household's usage or a promised saving. | Check current regulator requirements and provider/comparison-service terms for the offer. Mark commercial material clearly and keep emergency and debt help apart. |
| Tenant contents, landlord insurance and rent guarantee | After a neutral checklist, in a clearly distinct placement | Cover limits, eligibility, excess and exclusions. Keep insured-damage loss of rent distinct from arrears cover. Avoid personalised recommendations, rankings and underwriting promises. | Establish whether the activity is insurance distribution, a regulated introduction or a financial promotion. Verify the provider and appropriate permissions, approval or exemption before use. |
| Bank and e-money accounts | After the legal provider and protection checks | Explain safeguarding and where eligible deposit protection applies. Keep official register searches and neutral alternatives prominent. | Verify regulatory perimeter, provider permission and any permitted approval route. Check each proposed localized claim about protection. |
| International transfers | After the quote checklist | Compare the same payment, speed and receiving currency. Avoid affiliate pricing, promises of the best rate or guaranteed refunds. | Verify which permissions and promotion rules apply to the actual activity. Require provider approval for placement terms and pricing claims and clearly identify paid placement. |
| Credit reports and related services | Below all statutory free-report routes | Keep free access and disputed-data instructions prominent. Avoid implying users need a subscription, a product can guarantee a score, or a lender will approve an application. | Check whether the placement involves a regulated credit or credit-information activity, provider permission, approval and disclosure. |

An affiliate relationship does not itself supply regulatory approval. If a proposed placement is a regulated promotion by an unauthorised publisher, verify the statutory route before use. Where approval is required, confirm the approving firm's permission for that promotion. If content is advertising, identify it before readers engage with it. Avoid treating a generic site-wide statement as the only notice. The provider and any approver should see the exact copy, placement and language variants.
