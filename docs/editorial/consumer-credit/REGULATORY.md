# Editorial boundaries and future commercial activity

These Guides give practical consumer information without comparing individual products or gathering leads. Sources include examples of public issuer terms for evidence. There are no apply buttons, lender referrals, tracking links, product rankings or financial incentives. The Guides point readers in difficulty to MoneyHelper and regulated complaint channels.

If the owner later considers compensation for referrals, sponsored material, a credit eligibility tool, an application form or affiliate product comparisons, review the actual user journey and commercial arrangement before making changes. An invitation or inducement to engage in regulated activity in the course of business may be a financial promotion under FSMA section 21. Introducing people to lenders may be regulated credit broking, subject to applicable exclusions or exemptions. An educational label or advertising disclosure does not by itself resolve the financial promotion or permissions analysis. CONC 3 requirements can include clear, fair and non-misleading presentation and triggered representative examples. Check current legislation, Handbook rules, commencement and transitional provisions at that point; a consultation does not amend the Handbook.
