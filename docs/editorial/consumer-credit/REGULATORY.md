# Editorial boundaries and future commercial activity

These Guides give practical consumer information without comparing individual products or gathering leads. Sources include examples of public issuer terms for evidence. There are no apply buttons, lender referrals, tracking links, product rankings or financial incentives. The Guides point readers in difficulty to MoneyHelper and regulated complaint channels.

If the owner later considers compensation for referrals, sponsored material, a credit eligibility tool, an application form or affiliate product comparisons, review the actual user journey and commercial arrangement before making changes. An invitation or inducement to engage in regulated activity in the course of business may be a financial promotion under FSMA section 21. Introducing people to lenders may be regulated credit broking, subject to applicable exclusions or exemptions. An educational label or advertising disclosure does not by itself resolve the financial promotion or permissions analysis. CONC 3 requirements can include clear, fair and non-misleading presentation and triggered representative examples. Check the applicable Handbook rules and commencement provisions before any commercial launch. [FSMA section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/21) · [FCA credit broking rules](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/credit-broking-rules) · [FCA CONC 3](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/conc3)


On 27 November 2026 the FCA's final CONC 3 policy statement, PS26/24, superseded the consultation on those rule changes. FCA 2026/75 took effect on 23 November 2026, except Part 2 of Annex B, which takes effect on 23 February 2027. The FCA is still exploring changes to APR and cost disclosures. PS26/24 leaves the 51% representative APR definition used in the personal-loan Guide in place. These developments concern future commercial activity and do not require changes to the Guide examples.
