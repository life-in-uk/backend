# UK consumer credit coverage and decisions

Audited 27 November 2026. Production returned 81 Guides per locale, including the same six Money slugs, in zh-CN, zh-TW, en, ur and hi. Reviewed the six Money Guide bodies in all five locales, repository draft paths, the bank-account revision proposal, and currently open backend PRs. The protected publisher should check private revision history before any future import.

| Candidate | Existing coverage and gap | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Applying for a credit card | The credit history Guide already explains reports, eligibility checks, electoral registration and searches. The bank account Guide deals with bank-account identity checks and does not establish credit-card eligibility. | Add application and repayment workflow, lender-specific evidence and refusal, Section 75, chargeback and fraud. Link existing Guides for checking files and bank-account basics. |
| Credit builder cards | The credit history Guide already explains establishing a record without paying interest just to build credit. Rejections and first-card selection recur in the same reader journey. | Cover as a section in the application Guide, with limits, utilisation and risk. |
| Balance transfers | No existing Money Guide covers card transfers or cost comparisons. | Use a single comparison Guide for 0% offers, built around transfer fees, payment allocation and promotional expiry. |
| 0% purchase and money transfer cards | International transfers concern remittances, a different task. No existing Guide compares promotions with cash withdrawals. | Combine with balance transfers to help readers select the right transaction. |
| Rewards and cashback | Bank-account rewards and remittance cost comparisons are adjacent. Card reward value, exclusions and costs are uncovered. | Separate net-value Guide because the user decision is about costs on regular spending. |
| Applying for a personal loan | The credit history Guide explains lenders' assessments. No existing Guide compares actual terms or settlement and consolidation. | Separate personal loans Guide. |

Tax Guides, National Insurance and remittances were reviewed for overlap. None needs revisions for this release. The four slugs and all Guide-to-Guide links stay in Money & Finance. Frontend presentation is outside this draft PR.
