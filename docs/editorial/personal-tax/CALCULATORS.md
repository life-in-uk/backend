# Personal Tax calculator specification for owner review

The articles stay ordinary editorial Guides. A later implementation would place calculators in the existing frontend and keep reusable calculation functions independent of components and Markdown. The first release should use deterministic estimation with no account, tracking of financial inputs or HMRC write access. Output an estimate, computation breakdown, applicable year and jurisdiction, assumptions and links to the related Guide and official service.

## Architecture

- Require a selected UK tax year or event date. Rule records carry effective dates, jurisdiction, origin URL, exact legislative or HMRC locator, revision history and worked-example provenance. A published year is immutable. Corrections get a new rules revision with a documented diff. Planned or consultation figures stay outside the runtime table.
- Model residence or Scottish/Welsh taxpayer status explicitly. For annual employment and property calculations use the person's status and applicable tax year, rather than the work postcode or the address of the property. Record geographic scope for every component. Inheritance Tax and CGT also need event-specific dates and territorial scope checks.
- Use integer pence or an exact decimal representation, with rounding specified by component and an unrounded calculation trace. Keep separate subtotals for income tax, employee NI, reductions, allowable losses and credits. Reject impossible values and unsupported interactions. Do not return a reassuring zero for a case that was refused.
- Tests use independently hand-checked HMRC examples, year transitions, regional fixtures, threshold minus one penny / exactly / plus one penny and rounding fixtures. Test no double counting of allowances or losses. Validate schemas, complete transitions and unsupported-case messages in CI. Keep the first supported year 2026/27 only if the owner approves that scope and each rule is independently verified. Never silently fall back to another year.
- Provide accessible locale-aware currency input, an auditable breakdown, and mobile and Urdu RTL rendering. Only the interface, assumptions and report text are translated. A rules revision and input set must produce identical pence totals in every locale.

| Initial calculator | Version-one inputs and outputs | Explicit exclusions and test boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Salary take-home | Regular monthly gross, selected taxpayer status, standard allowance, typical employee Class 1 category A. Output annual Income Tax estimate, monthly NI and illustrative monthly take-home. Calculate monthly payroll thresholds separately from annual Income Tax; label calculation vs cumulative payroll deduction. | Exclude directors, multiple jobs, irregular pay, atypical codes, student loans and pension/salary-sacrifice until implemented. Test standard allowance taper and zero point, each regional band and monthly NI threshold. |
| Personally owned rental income | Share of UK residential receipts, eligible revenue expenses or eligible property allowance, finance costs, held-over property losses/finance costs and other income. Output taxable profit, incremental income tax and permitted finance-cost reduction. Screen alternative treatment eligibility before comparing. | Exclude company/partnership, overseas property, mixed use, non-residence, joint allocation disputes and complex finance cases. Test property allowance versus expenses, loss carry-forward, separate unused finance costs, reduction cap and jurisdiction. |
| CGT for property and investments | Event date, asset type, proceeds, acquisition and disposal costs, taxable income, verified current and brought-forward eligible losses, allowance and any user-supplied relief amount supported by the simple model. Output net gain, taxable gain, estimated CGT and reporting signpost. | Exclude share matching/pooling unless explicitly implemented, derivative/crypto complexity, trusts, cross-border cases and unsupported residence relief computations. Test same-year losses before the allowance versus limited use of earlier losses, basic-rate headroom, date transitions and property deadline routing. |
| Inheritance Tax estimate | Death date, simple UK estate, supported debts, straightforward exemption and allowanced amounts, qualifying home and direct descendants, transfer allowances supported by documented inputs. Output taxable estate and estimate with band and taper explanation where supported. | Exclude gift history that affects the estimate, trusts, foreign estates, residence transitions, business/agricultural relief, complex debts and reduced charitable rate unless explicitly modelled. Test bands, taper edge and full reduction, spouse exemptions, no qualifying home, and event-date boundaries. |

Owner decisions needed before implementation are the supported years, the first calculator to build, handling for the listed exclusions and who validates each law-to-code rule. A calculator cannot determine who must report, submit a return or calculate an authoritative HMRC liability.
