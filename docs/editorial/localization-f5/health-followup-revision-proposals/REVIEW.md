# F5 Health follow-up replacement proposals

**Draft · localization checked 27 November 2026.**

This batch follows the priority interpreter and bank-account drafts in [backend PR #92](https://github.com/life-in-uk/backend/pull/92). All three Health Guides retain their existing slugs, England NHS scope and useful Chinese material in zh-CN/zh-TW. Changes are local editorial proposals.

| Guide | What changes |
|---|---|
| Medicines and prescriptions | English, Urdu and Hindi now address medicines issued overseas generally, including brand and generic names, bringing medicines into the UK, and telling clinicians about herbal or traditional remedies. There is no assumption an overseas prescription will be continued. The Chinese examples stay, with the travel advice labelled as an example and a pointer to applicable UK requirements. |
| Healthcare for visiting parents | Verify the coverage territory, policy wording and any limits for pre-existing conditions. State the active ingredient and strength when medicines use a brand name unfamiliar in the UK. Ask the hospital for help in the language needed. These points no longer assume where the family came from, bought insurance or received a prescription. The Chinese versions keep their scoped insurance example. |
| IHS and NHS costs | The relevant England charging rules can depend on ordinary residence, applicable exemptions or reciprocal arrangements. Passport nationality alone does not establish entitlement. The Chinese versions keep the Chinese-passport example alongside this clarification. |

Rechecked the primary sources for the changed material. This is a localization review, with retained fee, timing, clinical and eligibility claims still subject to the normal release-day source review. No independent clinical, insurance or fluent human-language review is claimed.
