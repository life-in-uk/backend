# UK sources for the follow-up reviews

Checked 27 November 2026 for the scope and proposed editorial direction. Before each content revision, verify every retained clinical, eligibility, pricing or financial-protection claim against current primary evidence, then record the date and locator for the exact claim. These links do not constitute fresh sign-off on unchanged live medical or banking content.

| Topic | Official sources | Review before drafting |
|---|---|---|
| Interpretation | [NHS England commissioner guidance](https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/guidance-for-commissioners-interpreting-and-translation-services-in-primary-care/) · [NHS England community-language framework](https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/improvement-framework-community-language-translation-and-interpreting-services/) · [NHS 111](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/urgent-and-emergency-care-services/when-to-use-111/) | Verify specific settings, provision and charging, booking and consent, and safeguards when family or children offer to interpret. Primary-care guidance does not automatically describe every hospital's operational process. Check specific claims about availability. |
| Registering and translating resources | [NHS GP registration](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/how-to-register-with-a-gp-surgery/) · [NHS England PGM](https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/primary-medical-care-policy-and-guidance-manual-pgm/) · [You and your general practice](https://www.england.nhs.uk/gp/you-and-your-general-practice/) | Match existing evidence locators and excerpts to current PGM parts before re-quoting. Verify whether particular translation editions are present. |
| Records and vaccinations | [UKHSA immunisation with uncertain or incomplete records](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vaccination-of-individuals-with-uncertain-or-incomplete-immunisation) | Distinguish administrative tips, translation, clinical acceptance, local practice and clinical assessment. Do not infer equivalence of schedules. |
| Medicines | [NHS prescription charges](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/prescriptions/nhs-prescription-charges/) · [NHS England Pharmacy First](https://www.england.nhs.uk/primary-care/pharmacy/pharmacy-services/pharmacy-first/) · [GOV.UK taking medicine in or out](https://www.gov.uk/take-medicine-in-or-out-uk) · [Controlled medicines](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/controlled-drugs-personal-licences) | Verify charges, exceptions, routes of access and travel thresholds separately. |
| Visitors and IHS | [GOV.UK immigration health charge](https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application) · [DHSC visitor charges in England](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/nhs-cost-recovery-overseas-visitors/charging-overseas-visitors-in-england-guidance-for-providers-of-nhs-services) | Separate status, exemptions, reciprocal rights, visitor insurance and ordinary patient charges. |
| Clinical navigation and dentistry | [NHS 999](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/urgent-and-emergency-care-services/when-to-call-999/) · [Emergency dentist](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/dentists/how-to-find-an-nhs-dentist-in-an-emergency/) · [NHS dental costs](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/dentists/how-much-nhs-dental-treatment-costs/) | Preserve treatment urgency and emergency wording. Date any costs or charge bands. |
| Bank identification and protection | [Money Laundering Regulations, regulation 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/692/regulation/28) · [Payment Accounts Regulations, regulation 19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/2038/regulation/19) · [MoneyHelper ID guidance](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/blog/savings/how-to-beat-id-issues-when-opening-a-bank-account) · [FSCS deposit protection](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/banks-building-societies/) | Keep regulatory requirements separate from each provider's way of checking identity. Check basic bank account exceptions and the latest deposit limits. Separate deposit protection and safeguarding. Check the actual provider's eligibility terms before naming accepted documents. |
